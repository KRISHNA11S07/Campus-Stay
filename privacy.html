Privacy Policy

Draft for operator review before release.

Accounts and owner applications

CampusStay uses Supabase Auth for sign-in. Signing in with Google shares the Google account information you authorize. A profile stores account details and preferences. Owner applicants provide their name, property relationship, address and a reference that a reviewer can check. Supabase supplies the confirmed account contact. An applicant and authorized administrators can read their application. Please keep identity documents, bank details and government ID numbers out of free-text fields.

Listings, contacts and location

Listings under review are available to the submitting owner and reviewers. Approved available listings can show the property description, photos, rent, area, chosen public contact details and an approximate pin. Exact address and coordinates are stored separately. Use area-only location to omit the public pin. Property photos may themselves reveal a location.

Enquiries, bookings and reviews

A student, the relevant property owner and authorized administrators can read an enquiry. A booking request records the student and property relationship. Published reviews are visible to visitors. Use a private enquiry for contact details. Calling or opening WhatsApp takes the conversation outside CampusStay.

Service providers and device storage

GitHub Pages serves the website. Supabase handles authentication, database records and private file storage. Images can be delivered through temporary signed links. Pages also request assets from font, image and script providers. Viewing an embedded map contacts Google Maps. These requests can disclose connection information such as your IP address. Your browser stores session information, display preferences and cookie choices.

Retention, access and deletion

Accounts, listings, applications, enquiry history and moderation records may remain in the database following a request. An administrator reviews deletion requests and any records that must be retained. A draft submission clears its saved form payload and retains a submission receipt. No fixed retention schedule has been published. You can edit profile details in Settings or submit a request about access, correction or deletion through Help.
