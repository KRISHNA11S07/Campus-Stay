Using CampusStay

CampusStay helps students discover accommodation near KIT’s College of Engineering, Kolhapur. Keep your sign-in information private. Provide accurate information and respect the privacy of students, owners and applicants.

Listing authority and review

Only list accommodation you own or have permission to manage. Provide an independently checkable authority reference and photos you may publish. Administrators can request clarification, decline an application, suspend owner access or withdraw a listing. Publication records a moderation decision. Inspect the property and confirm availability, costs and rental terms with the owner before making a commitment.

Enquiries and booking requests

A message or booking request expresses interest. The website does not collect rent or deposits. Agree viewing and rental arrangements directly with the other party. Reviews must relate to an eligible booking and your own experience.

Conduct and support

Keep personal documents and payment credentials out of listings, reviews and reports. Use Help to report a misleading listing, abuse, privacy concerns or incorrect information. Use Settings to request account deletion.
